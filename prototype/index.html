Built for Trust

Every feature designed to protect both parties in every transaction

Managed Escrow

Funds held securely via Stripe Connect. Released only when both parties confirm delivery and acceptance — or through structured dispute resolution.

  • Manual capture (up to 90 days)
  • Platform-controlled release
  • Automatic refund on timeout

Progressive Verification

Three-tier identity system builds trust incrementally. From email-only to government ID + selfie to full business KYB.

  • Basic: Email verified
  • Verified: ID + selfie (Stripe Identity)
  • Business: Docs + KYB

Structured Disputes

Evidence-based resolution with platform reviewers. Auto-compiled chargeback representment using deal room artifacts.

  • Evidence submission portal
  • 5-day SLA for decisions
  • Auto-representment to Stripe

Multi-Currency

Deal in USD, EUR, or GBP. Sellers choose settlement currency. Transparent FX with 1% buffer.

  • USD, EUR, GBP at launch
  • Seller picks payout currency
  • Real-time Stripe FX rates

Legal Agreements

Auto-generated agreements with dual e-signatures, SHA-256 content hashing, and PDF audit trail.

  • Template-based generation
  • Dual acceptance required
  • Immutable audit log

Marketplace Ready

Service listings with search, categories, reviews, and seller analytics — all integrated with escrow.

  • Full-text search
  • Reviews & ratings
  • Seller dashboard
